Find Naturist and Nudist Beaches Worldwide

BeachScout is a free map for finding naturist, nudist and clothing-optional beaches around the world. It also includes naturist resorts and campsites, beach conditions, nearby accommodation, parking and practical local information.

Search around any town, search near your current location, or browse naturist places country by country. BeachScout uses open geographic data and clearly distinguishes official, customary and other mapped naturist locations instead of guessing.

What BeachScout helps you find

Naturist and nudist beaches

Find official naturist beaches, customary naturist beaches and other clothing-optional places recorded in OpenStreetMap.

  • Coastal, lake and river beaches
  • Country-wide naturist maps
  • Nearby search around your location
  • Clear labels based on mapped data

Naturist resorts and campsites

BeachScout also shows mapped naturist resorts, holiday villages, campsites and swimming facilities so you can plan more than a day trip.

  • Resorts and villages
  • Naturist campsites
  • Swimming pools and lidos where mapped
  • Address, telephone and website where available

Beach conditions

For supported locations, BeachScout can show practical conditions before you set off.

  • Air and sea temperature
  • Waves and wind
  • UV and rain
  • Sunset time

Stay nearby and parking

Open a beach to look for accommodation and public parking nearby.

  • Hotels, guest houses, apartments and campsites
  • Postal address and phone where mapped
  • Nearest public parking
  • Google Maps links for navigation

Why BeachScout exists

Beach information is scattered across maps, travel sites and local knowledge. BeachScout brings useful open geographic information together on one map and makes it easier to answer practical questions: where is the beach, is it naturist, what are the conditions, where can I stay, and where can I park?

The app is independent, free to use and does not require an account. Coverage depends on the quality of open data available for each location, so local signs and official information should always take priority.
