Independent software developer · creator of ScoutPlatform

I build ScoutPlatform, a set of free exploration tools made from open geographic data. The idea is simple: the data needed to find a dog-friendly beach, a free parking spot, or a charger that actually fits your range is already public — it just is not usable. Each Scout app turns one slice of it into a map you can use without an account, a subscription, or a tracker.

Everything here is built and maintained by one person. The project is independent and community-oriented, and the goal is to keep it free or inexpensive to use.

Applications

  • BeachScout — beaches worldwide, with dog access, naturist locations, sea conditions, accommodation and parking. Also on Google Play.
  • RoadScout — cultural and natural places along a driving route.
  • ParkingScout — city parking, free versus paid, with EV charging. Early access.
  • EVchargeScout — EV road-trip charging with range safety margins. Early access.
  • HotelScout — accommodation along a route, not around a point. Early access.

MountainScout and HeritageScout are planned.
