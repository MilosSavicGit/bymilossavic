ScoutPlatform

Open tools for exploring and documenting places around the world.

Vision

ScoutPlatform helps people discover places, document local knowledge and share information that can complement open geographic projects such as OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons.

The long-term goal is to create practical, community-powered tools that make it easier to explore the world and preserve useful knowledge about real places.

About

ScoutPlatform is created by Milos Savic, an independent developer based in Belgrade, Serbia.

This project started with RoadScout and BeachScout and is evolving into a reusable platform for open geospatial exploration and community documentation.

The applications are intended to be simple, useful and free or very inexpensive to operate.
