ScoutPlatform

Simple exploration tools built with open data and community knowledge.

ScoutPlatform is an independent project by Milos Savic. Its applications help people discover useful and interesting places around the world through open geographic data, clear maps and responsible community contributions.

What you can search

BeachScout started as a beach map. It has grown into a way of asking practical questions about a stretch of coast before you drive to it.
New in 2.3

🐕 Can I bring my dog?

Most beach apps cannot answer this. BeachScout can, wherever a local mapper has recorded it. Open Dog beaches in this country and you get every beach in that country that states a rule, on one map, colour-coded:

Dogs allowed Dedicated dog beach On a lead Dogs not allowed Changes with the season

Tap any dot for the full beach page: conditions, accommodation nearby, parking and coordinates. A ban is shown just as clearly as permission — knowing where you cannot go saves a wasted journey.

Coverage depends entirely on what local mappers have recorded, and it is uneven. Germany, Croatia, Spain and the Netherlands are well mapped; many countries have almost nothing yet. A beach with no dog tag is not a beach that bans dogs — it is a beach nobody has recorded. BeachScout never guesses, and always tells you to check local signs.

📍 Detailed search

Search 10 km around any town in the world, or 10 km around wherever you are standing.

  • Beaches, lake and river swimming places
  • Naturist beaches, resorts and campsites
  • Beach resorts, lidos and swimming pools
  • Local names translated to English where the data allows

🏖 Naturist places, country by country

The whole of a country's naturist coastline on one map, in the same colours the search uses.

  • Official and customary naturist beaches
  • Naturist resorts, villages and campsites
  • Naturist swimming pools
  • Browse as a map or as a grouped list

🌊 Beach conditions

What the water is doing today, at that beach, before you set off.

  • Air and sea temperature
  • Wave height and wind
  • UV index and rain
  • Sunset time

🏨 Stay nearby and parking

Practical detail for actually getting there and staying the night.

  • Hotels, guest houses, apartments and campsites within 3 km
  • Full postal address in the local convention
  • Telephone number and official website
  • Nearest public parking, with walking directions

Coming next

ScoutPlatform is designed to grow into a wider ecosystem of focused exploration tools.
In development

🚗 ParkingScout

A map of a city's parking that makes clear which of it is free — the question every driver actually has.

  • Public car parks, garages and street parking
  • Free, paid and unknown shown honestly apart
  • Park-and-ride sites on the edge of the city
  • Capacity, opening hours and accessible spaces

Groundwork underway. Parking data has been measured across seven cities to establish where it is complete enough to be trusted. Unrecorded parking will never be shown as free.

In development

Community contributions

BeachScout is designed to become more useful as local users add knowledge.

Users may contribute local beach information, including up to five photos and a short description of up to 300 characters. Contributions are reviewed before publication.

Contributors remain the owners of their photographs and descriptions. BeachScout receives permission only to store, moderate and display those contributions as part of the service.

About the creator

Independent project by Milos Savic.

BeachScout and ScoutPlatform were created by Milos Savic. BeachScout began with a simple idea: to build a worldwide map useful to the naturist community, while also serving anyone looking for beaches and swimming places around the world.

The project is independent, community-oriented and built with the goal of remaining free or inexpensive to use.
