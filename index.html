Three steps to a richer drive

Plan before you go. Cultural Map does the scouting so you arrive knowing exactly what's worth a stop.

1

Enter your route

Type where you're starting and where you're headed. Cultural Map maps the drive and the routes you could take.

2

Scan the corridor

It searches a corridor along your route for cultural attractions, in the order you'll reach them — with distance and detour for each.

3

Choose your stops

Read a short description of each place, in the local language or translated to English, and pick the ones worth your time.

Why I built this

Every trip planner I tried showed me the same handful of famous places — and nothing of the strange, wonderful, lesser-known spots that make a drive memorable. So I built the tool I wanted: one that scouts the whole route, in any country, in any language, and surfaces the places worth pulling over for.

It's free, it's a labour of love, and it gets better every time someone uses it.

— Miloš Savić
