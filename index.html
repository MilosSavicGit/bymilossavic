ScoutPlatform

Simple exploration tools built with open data and community knowledge.

ScoutPlatform is an independent project by Milos Savic. Its applications help people discover useful and interesting places around the world through open geographic data, clear maps and responsible community contributions.

Community contributions

BeachScout is designed to become more useful as local users add knowledge.

Users may contribute local beach information, including up to five photos and a short description of up to 300 characters. Contributions are reviewed before publication.

Contributors remain the owners of their photographs and descriptions. BeachScout receives permission only to store, moderate and display those contributions as part of the service.

About the creator

Independent project by Milos Savic.

BeachScout and ScoutPlatform were created by Milos Savic. BeachScout began with a simple idea: to build a worldwide map useful to the naturist community, while also serving anyone looking for beaches and swimming places around the world.

The project is independent, community-oriented and built with the goal of remaining free or inexpensive to use.
